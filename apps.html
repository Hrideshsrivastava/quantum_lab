📖 What connects these applications?

Quantum Superposition

Every application here relies on a particle or photon existing in multiple states simultaneously — a resource with no classical equivalent.

Optical Interference

MZI sensing and the bomb detector both exploit constructive/destructive interference to encode information with extreme precision.

No-Cloning Theorem

QKD security rests on the impossibility of copying an unknown quantum state — any eavesdropper inevitably disturbs and reveals herself.

Measurement Back-Action

The aerospace and bridge SHM tools leverage ultra-sensitive MZI phase measurement. The bomb detector turns back-action into a feature.
